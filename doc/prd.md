**Website, Learning Management System (LMS), AI Platform & Mobile Ecosystem**

**Theme**
**Leadership. Strategy. Transformation.**

---

# Executive Summary

School of Growth Global is a world-class digital leadership and business transformation institution dedicated to raising transformational leaders, entrepreneurs, professionals, organizations, and governments through practical education, mentorship, strategic consulting, and innovation.

The platform will serve as the institution's primary digital ecosystem where learners can discover courses, enroll in programs, attend live and recorded classes, receive mentorship, earn globally verifiable certifications, participate in communities, and access AI-powered learning assistance.

The solution will include:

* Public Website
* Learning Management System
* Student Portal
* Lecturer Portal
* Mentor Marketplace
* Corporate Training Portal
* AI Learning Assistant
* Admin Management System
* Mobile Application (Android & iOS)
